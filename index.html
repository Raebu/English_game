DETECTIVE HANDBOOK

Noun Power Guide

🎒
Common Nouns

General names for people, places or things, such as dog, school or bicycle.

👑
Proper Nouns

Special names for particular people, places, days or brands. They begin with a capital letter, like London, Friday or Disney.

💭
Abstract Nouns

Feelings, ideas and qualities you cannot touch, such as joy, kindness or bravery.
