Privacy & learner data

Short version: Genius Academy is designed to keep ordinary learner progress on the device. The optional AI tutor receives only the information needed to answer the current learning question.

Information stored on this device

The web app can store a learner-selected display name, scores, mission progress, skill/mastery information, rewards and parent-configured learning settings in browser storage. This lets progress survive a page refresh without requiring an account.

Optional AI tutor

If the AI tutor is enabled, Genius Academy may send the learner's current question, school year and up to five priority-skill labels/mastery percentages to the configured AI provider so it can produce an age-appropriate explanation.

The learner's display name is not sent to the AI provider. The tutor is instructed not to ask for or infer a full name, contact details, school or other identifying information. If the provider is not configured or is unavailable, the app reports the tutor as unavailable rather than pretending a canned answer came from AI.

Advertising and tracking

The current repository does not include an advertising SDK or a behavioural analytics integration. If that changes, this notice and the privacy review must be updated before release.

Android

The Android app disables Android backup for learner state in the application manifest. Release builds still require device testing before publication.

Parents and carers

Parents and carers should avoid entering a child's full name, school, address, contact details or other identifying information into the AI tutor. A future production release must complete the applicable child-data, transparency, retention and provider review before external launch.

Engineering status

This page describes the current technical behaviour of the repository. It is not a substitute for the final legal/privacy notice or formal compliance approval.
